## Red Alert (bonus, from scatters)

You are Enterprise-D (model in art) being attacked by a fleet of Klingon ships in waves. They can cloak/uncloak, and destroying them reveals either a monetary reward, or free spins (with bonuses). You take damage when they fire back, at the end of each wave your health is restored and a new wave begins. Up to 5 waves total, with increasing rewards for each wave completed. Completing all 5 waves triggers something special.
