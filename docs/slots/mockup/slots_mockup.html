To sign off

  1. Art set: hand-drawn vector or FLUX painted (and, for FLUX, which candidate for any symbol you'd rather swap).
  2. Stakes: one ladder: 25p, 50p, 75p, £1, £1.50, £2.50, £5, £10, £25. Buy Bonus costs 45× the bet (£11.25 to £1,125).
  3. Return: the game about 91.5%, the progressives 3.75% more, for about 95% in total (the real game is 94.47%). The Buy Bonus returns 93.7%, as on the real game.
  4. Jackpot Pick: seeds Mini £10, Minor £50, Major £500, Grand £5,000. Each bet puts 0.25%, 0.25%, 0.5% and 1% into them. Won about once per £1,500, £10,000, £150,000 and £2,000,000 staked. The trigger chance scales with the bet, so every stake gets the same return.
  5. Rule readings where the rules sheet is silent: Boots pays like Whiskey; Repeat Win pays the same win 2 to 5 times; at most one Double per trail; a Lightning Roll passes over signposts.
  6. The trail: 120 spaces with 20 snakes, 2 hearts, 3 each of forward, back and crates, 2 lightnings, and a 20% chance of a Spin Upgrade and 40% of a Double. About 6 shots a bonus; about 1 in 250 trails reach the prizepot.
  7. Big wins: BIG at 10× the bet, MEGA at 25×, EPIC at 50×. Autoplay runs 10, 25, 50 or 100 spins and stops on any bonus or a win of 25× or more.
  8. The cabinet: its look, the 4 orientations and the 10-cabinet bank in the south-east corner. A player sitting at a cabinet whose screen faces away from the camera would be hidden behind it, so that cabinet fades while someone sits there (as on the floor view). Keep that, or leave them hidden?
  9. Sounds aren't in this mockup. They'll be picked on the admin page's Sounds tab, as for the other games.

How it plays

  • Random features can hit any spin. Before the reels stop: Random Wilds (1 to 4 shot onto the reels), Wild Reels (1 to 3 whole reels), Colossal Reels (2×2, 3×3 or 4×4 symbols) and a Multiplier (2× to 5× on line wins). After they stop: Symbol Upgrade (one symbol moves up the chain), Repeat Win and Instant Win (1× to 200× the bet from the barrel).
  • Trail Bonus: 3 or more Wagons. Fire the pistol and the armadillo moves 1 to 6 spaces along the trail, collecting what it lands on. A Snake ends it unless you hold a Heart. Reach the end for the 2,000× prizepot.
  • Combo Free Games: 3 or more Pistols. Three free games where a random feature is guaranteed every spin, often several at once.
  • Jackpot Pick: can start on any paid spin. Pick coins until three match; that jackpot is yours. The meters are shared by every slot in the casino.
  • Leaving mid-feature: in the game, a bonus you walk away from is played out for you and paid. The next player finds a fresh cabinet.
