How it behaves

  • The top-left shows cash in hand, which is what you bet with. The bank balance only appears at the ATM.
  • Withdraw only while you hold under £500. The limit is £250 a time, or £2,000 once the bank is at £2,500 or more. Amounts are in £50 notes. Going above £500 on hand is fine (£400 + £250).
  • The bank can go overdrawn without limit. While it's overdrawn you can withdraw once every 30 minutes. The withdrawal that first takes you overdrawn is allowed, and it starts the clock.
  • Deposit any amount you're holding, at any time.
  • Use it on foot only: walk up, then E, the prompt, or a click on the machine. Esc leaves it.
  • Top up is gone. Existing players keep their balance as cash in hand and get £1,000 in the bank.
