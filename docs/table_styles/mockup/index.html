    What stays put

    • The felt's outline, the rail's outer edge and collision: CasinoTable.outline() / grown().
    • Every betting spot's centre and size, every card position, and the shuffler, shoe and discard rack positions.
    • The chip tray's place. The ledge only replaces the felt behind the dealer's cards; the gap to the cards stays 5.9.
    • Roulette and Craps keep today's look and chips until they're moved onto a style later.

    Turn on Fixed layout to see those positions drawn over any design.
