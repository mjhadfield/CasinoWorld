Try it

You're in the middle seat. Pick a chip, click the glowing spot to bet, then Ready. Right-click a spot to take a chip back. Keys: 1–5 chips, Space ready, Backspace undo, R rebet. At Craps: R or Space rolls, T takes the dice, W working.

What's different

Show the letters on the table
  • AChips in the middle, bigger, in the table's own style. The picked one lifts with a gold ring. A chip you can't afford dims.
  • BUndo and Rebet on one line, just right of the chips, so everything that changes your bet is in one place. Rebet turns into Clear once chips are down, since the two never apply at once. Auto rebet is a small switch beside it.
  • COne button to commit, just left of the chips. It reads Sit out until you bet, then Ready.
  • DMoney on the left, large and centred in the bar: your balance, and what's on the felt. Wins and losses flash beside the balance.
  • EOne line says what's happening, with a gold line draining for the countdown. Your hand's name goes here too. Pending changes and votes sit above it.
  • FDecisions replace the chips while they're open, since you can't bet then anyway, and name the amount: Play £25, Double £25.
  • GStand up and Paytable move to the top corner, away from Ready, so leaving is never a slip of the finger.
  • HThe table's name and who's seated sit top left.
  • IThe jackpot has its own panel under it, counting up live. It lights up and says so when your £1 is in.

At Roulette and Craps

  • RoRoulette keeps the same bar. Ready spins sooner, and the chips stay live through Final bets! until No more bets. Hovering the layout lights up the numbers a bet covers and names it and its payout on the line above the chips.
  • CrCraps has no Ready or Rebet, since bets stay up from roll to roll. Left of the chips are the dice: Take dice, or Pass dice and Roll when it's your roll. Working takes Auto rebet's place, and Clear takes down everything that can come down.
  • +The line above the chips carries the point and who's shooting ("Point is 6 · Ivy shooting · Dice out in 4"), and the bet under the pointer, with "off on the come-out" where that applies.
