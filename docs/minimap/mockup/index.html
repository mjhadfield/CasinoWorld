How it behaves

  • It shows your room, and every room one doorway away. Rooms joined by an archway count as one.
  • When that's too big to fit, it pans with you instead of shrinking past readable.
  • A door to a room further away gets a tab with the room's name.
  • A table with no free seat is drawn faint. Other real players are dots; NPCs aren't shown.
  • At a turned table the map turns with the view, so what's behind you on screen is behind you on the map.
  • It starts folded to the map button, top right; a click opens it. ✕ folds it again. Settings › Show the minimap (on by default) removes it altogether.
  • + and − zoom it, from everything-fits up to a close look round you, and the game remembers your zoom.
  • Find lists what's in the casino and picks the nearest by walking distance, preferring a free seat. The route clears when you get there, sit down, or press ✕.
