A second sign on every card table, standing at the other end of the dealer's ledge from the progressive jackpot. It shows the table's limits for the main bet and the side bets, which are set per table on the Floor plan. Pick a style and a game below and every picture follows.

Setting the limits

On the Floor plan, a card table's panel gets two new rows. The numbers here drive every sign on this page, so try a few. Tables with nothing set use the defaults: £5 to £500 on the main bet, £1 to £50 on side bets.

Whole pounds, £1 to £10,000. The Jackpot bet stays £1 whatever these say.

  • Over the maximum is refused at the chip. The chip doesn't go down and the toast says "The table maximum on the Ante is £500."
  • Under the minimum is allowed while you build the bet (you build a £5 Ante from £1 chips), but Ready is refused while one is short, and when betting closes a short bet is handed back and not played. If that's the main bet, you sit the hand out.
  • Play, Raise and doubles aren't limited. They're set by the Ante. Flat bets (the £1 Jackpot, Let It Ride's £1 Bonus) stay exact.
  • Main bet is the Ante (Blackjack's Bet; Let It Ride's three bets; Hold'em's Ante and Blind; Baccarat's Player and Banker). Side bets are the rest, Baccarat's Tie included.
  • Changing limits mid-session leaves bets already down alone. A Rebet that no longer fits is refused, and Auto rebet turns itself off and says why.
  • NPCs fit the table: they bet within the limits, and won't sit where they can't afford the minimum for a few hands (they get up if they drop below it).
