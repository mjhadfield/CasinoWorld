Try it

  1. Click a glowing chair to sit down.
  2. Pick a game from the dealer's menu.
  3. Click the spots on the felt to bet, then Ready.
  4. Click the dealer to change game. Mid-hand, it waits for the hand to end.
  5. Stand up and the table goes back to the Master Table after a while.

The rules this demo follows

  • Anyone seated can change the game, but only between hands. Bets on the felt are handed back first.
  • Other players get 10 s to agree. Saying nothing counts as yes.
  • Let It Ride and High Card Flush seat five: they need a free seat, and the chairs slide to fit.
  • Rounds are a short stand-in: real layouts, but wins and losses are random. Betting closes after 12 s (15 s in the game).
