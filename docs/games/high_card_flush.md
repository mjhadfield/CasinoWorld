# High Card Flush (`hcf_1`)

Five seats, spread out for room.

## Bets

Ante, plus the Flush, Straight Flush and £1 Jackpot side bets.

## The deal

Seven cards each. Your own are fanned with your flush first.

## Decisions

Everyone at once: **fold**, or **raise** 1x (2x with a 5-card flush, 3x with 6 or 7). Running out of time
folds.
- **Raise:** your flush goes face down in front of you, and the other cards are set aside and swept. The
  dealer's hand is shown the same way: the flush slides forward and the rest is swept.
- **Fold:** the hand is swept away unseen with its Ante and side bets, the Flush bonus included (a table
  rule; v2 paid it). The exception is a straight flush of three or more with a Straight Flush bet or the
  Jackpot riding: those cards are tucked under the Straight Flush bet, as with a Prime at Three Card Poker,
  and still pay. A raised hand tucks a straight flush in another suit the same way.

## A second box
With a second box (README, Betting at the card tables), Box 2's seven cards turn over for you, sorted with its
flush first, once Box 1 has raised or folded. Its own flush sets how far it can raise.

## Settling

The longest flush wins, then the high cards. The dealer qualifies with a 3-card flush, 9-high; if not, the
Ante pays and the Raise pushes.

## NPCs

They follow Mousseau's strategy (raise any 4+ flush or a 3-card 10-8-6+, always the most allowed).
Simulated: 2.64% ± 0.11% (published 2.71%).
