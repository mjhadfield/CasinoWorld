# Let It Ride (`lir_1`)

Five seats, spread out for room.

## Bets

Three equal bets, 1, 2 and £ (a chip on any one sets all three). Side bets: the £1 Bonus, the 3 Card Bonus
and the £1 Jackpot.

## The deal

Three cards each (private) and two community cards.

## Decisions

After your three cards, **let it ride** (**R**) or **pull back** (**P**) bet 1; after the first community
card, bet 2. Pulled bets come straight back. Running out of time pulls back. The £ always plays.

## A second box
With a second box (README, Betting at the card tables), Box 2's cards turn over for you once Box 1 has decided
its first bet, and on each bet Box 1 decides before Box 2.

## Settling

A pair of 10s or better pays every bet still up (v2's paytable).

## NPCs

They follow the Wizard of Odds' strategy; a Gambler lets everything ride. A Casual's slip
(`npc_slips.py`) pulls a draw the chart rides, or rides a small pair, three suited cards or an inside
straight draw; a paying hand always rides. Simulated (the chart): 3.27% ± 0.14% of one unit with v2's
paytable.
